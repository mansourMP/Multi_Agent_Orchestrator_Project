# FOUNDER MEMO

Date: 2026-04-09

## Product Statement

Empyralis is an **AI Operating System for authorized digital work**.

The product is not a chatbot, not a workflow toy, and not an app marketplace with an AI wrapper.

The product is:

- one visible intelligence: **Sage**
- one execution substrate: **Harness**
- one distribution layer: **Store**

The user should feel one relationship:

- I talk to Sage
- Sage remembers context
- Sage delegates to specialists
- Sage can act across devices and services
- I can watch, approve, pause, or kill anything

The clearest external sentence is:

**Empyralis is the AI OS people trust to actually do work across their devices and services, under their control.**

## Product Shape

### What users see

- Sage
- the cockpit
- approvals
- installed specialists
- mobile oversight

### What users do not need to think about

- DAGs
- workflow graphs
- runtime internals
- child-run topology
- storage or compiler concepts

### First principle

Sage is the hero.  
Everything else is subordinate.

## Pricing Thesis

We do not charge like a commodity chat wrapper.

We charge for:

- autonomy
- execution power
- device reach
- durable background work
- safety and control

### Consumer / Prosumer

- `Free`
  - limited Sage usage
  - limited memory/context
  - limited installs
  - no meaningful background execution

- `Pro`
  - Sage + specialist installs
  - local desktop/mobile control
  - larger context and memory
  - better execution limits

- `Operator`
  - durable background runs
  - advanced autonomy
  - more devices
  - premium first-party specialists
  - stronger cockpit and control features

- `Creator / Studio`
  - private template publishing
  - advanced agent configuration
  - future monetization path into the Store

### Enterprise

Enterprise pricing should be based on:

- platform contract
- seat count
- execution/runtime volume
- compliance, fleet control, and audit requirements

### Marketplace

The Store should take a platform fee because it provides:

- distribution
- billing
- execution harness
- policy/approval infrastructure
- versioning
- auditability

The default take rate can be `30%` as long as the platform is genuinely valuable.

## Moat

The moat is not “we have agents.”
Everyone will say that.

The moat is the combination of:

- one Master Agent identity
- real device control on authorized machines
- durable runs
- mobile remote oversight
- approvals and hard kill
- structured audit
- installable specialists
- one canonical turn and run engine

In plain terms:

**Most AI products can answer. Very few can safely act, delegate, persist, and stay under control across time and devices.**

That is the moat.

## Launch Narrative

The launch story should be narrow, not broad.

We are not launching:

- a generic workflow builder
- a broad enterprise automation suite
- a developer marketplace first

We are launching:

**Sage, your AI operating system that can actually do real work across your computer, cloud tools, and background agents, while staying visible and killable.**

### Demo sequence

1. User asks Sage to do something real.
2. Sage chooses the right specialist.
3. Execution starts on the right target.
4. The cockpit shows what is happening live.
5. User approves or kills from web or mobile.
6. Result comes back cleanly.
7. The behavior can be saved or installed as a reusable capability.

That is the story investors, users, and enterprise buyers will remember.

## What We Are Not Building Right Now

This matters more than the ambition list.

We are **not** building right now:

- a user-facing drag-and-drop workflow-first product
- a marketplace-first product
- ten equal top-level agent personalities
- a dashboard full of unrelated tabs
- custom enterprise complexity before Sage feels inevitable
- a pricing model centered on tiny token metering
- a broad DIY builder for non-technical users before the default product is coherent
- a full “agent civilization simulator” surface

We are also not spending the next sprint on:

- rewriting the core runtime
- major architecture churn
- speculative platform abstractions with no demo impact

## What We Build Next

The next product priority order is:

1. Make Sage feel elite.
2. Make the cockpit feel trustworthy and powerful.
3. Make first-party specialists installable and legible.
4. Make local execution feel magical but controlled.
5. Make the Store feel like expansion, not fragmentation.

## Final Position

Empyralis wins if it feels like:

- one AI OS
- one command center
- one relationship
- one safety model
- many invisible workers

The mistake to avoid is letting users see the plumbing before they believe the magic.

The product is not “all the components.”
The product is **Sage**.

Everything else exists to make Sage real.
