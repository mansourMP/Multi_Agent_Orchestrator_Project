# Overnight Benchmark Study

Date: 2026-04-09

## Goal

Study how top-tier product surfaces handle:

- primary vs. secondary actions
- density without clutter
- sidebars and panels
- notification systems
- motion and transitions
- copy economy

Benchmark set:

- Notion
- Linear
- OpenAI Platform
- Google Material 3
- Anthropic
- ElevenLabs

## Notion

Reference links:

- [Getting Started with Notion](https://www.notion.so/notion/Getting-Started-with-Notion-f0e1a6d326d84d6984d948da96965045)
- [Workspace and sidebar guidance](https://www.notion.so/a752c2568e9b4aecb7d192e1d1de90d3)
- [Side peek](https://www.notion.so/Open-any-page-in-side-peek-b01aca54b2f440c294f77f05ac0d8871)

Key traits:

- quiet sidebar, stronger content plane
- very light chrome
- context-preserving side panels
- low-noise metadata and labels
- primary actions are rare, which makes them credible

What to copy:

- dimmer navigation
- side-peek mindset for supporting context
- quieter typography
- aggressive reduction of decorative emphasis

## Linear

Reference links:

- [Linear changelog](https://linear.app/changelog)
- [Linear Inbox](https://linear.app/docs/inbox)
- [Linear Docs](https://linear.app/docs)

Key traits:

- high information density without feeling crowded
- excellent hierarchy between navigation, list content, and detail panes
- command and keyboard surfaces feel first-class
- inbox and notifications are structured, not noisy
- recent UI refreshes consistently push toward calmer chrome and stronger content focus

What to copy:

- dense but legible list rows
- calmer sidebars
- stronger content headers
- operational surfaces that feel exact rather than decorative

## OpenAI Platform

Reference links:

- [API Usage Dashboard](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10478918-api-usage-dashboard)
- [Usage Dashboard legacy notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8554956-understanding-the-usage-dashboard)
- [Prompt generation guide](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/prompt-generation/schemas)

Key traits:

- platform surfaces are scoped by project and organization
- usage data is presented with explicit filtering and ownership context
- action hierarchy is sparse
- the UI stays technical without feeling visually messy

What to copy:

- operational honesty
- filtering only where the data model is strong enough
- clean admin surfaces with low visual drama

## Google / Material 3

Reference links:

- [Buttons](https://m3.material.io/components/buttons/overview)
- [Navigation drawer](https://m3.material.io/components/navigation-drawer/overview)
- [Snackbar](https://m3.material.io/components/snackbar/overview)

Key traits:

- systematic button hierarchy
- predictable component semantics
- restrained motion
- notifications are short, scoped, and clearly temporary

What to copy:

- stricter action tiers
- more disciplined snackbar/notice behavior
- motion used for orientation, not flair

## Anthropic

Reference links:

- [Projects](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9517075-what-are-projects)
- [Artifacts](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9487310-what-are-artifacts-and-how-do-i-use-them)
- [Artifacts for app creation](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11649427-use-artifacts-to-visualize-and-create-ai-apps-without-ever-writing-a-line-of-code)

Key traits:

- core chat relationship remains central
- artifacts live beside the conversation, not as a separate product universe
- structured output is visually distinct from assistant prose
- advanced capability is hidden behind a calm surface

What to copy:

- preserve Sage as the emotional center
- keep structured interventions separate from assistant text
- use side-by-side context intelligently instead of adding more top-level pages

## ElevenLabs

Reference links:

- [Agents dashboard](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/conversational-ai/dashboard)
- [Projects / Studio overview](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/product/projects/overview)
- [Studio](https://elevenlabs.io/studio)
- [Knowledge base dashboard](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/conversational-ai/customization/knowledge-base/dashboard)

Key traits:

- creator tooling feels premium and modular
- dashboards are structured without looking enterprise-boring
- reusable assets, libraries, and configuration are kept inside one coherent frame

What to copy:

- premium switchboard feeling for agent installs
- modular cards that still feel like one product
- cleaner catalog and asset-library treatment

## Comparative Summary

### Best at calm density

- Linear
- Notion

### Best at structured AI interaction

- Anthropic
- OpenAI Platform

### Best at systematic component behavior

- Google Material 3

### Best at premium creator tooling

- ElevenLabs

## Direct Implications for Empyralis

### Sidebar

Copy from:

- Notion
- Linear

Rule:

- sidebar is infrastructure, not the show

### Chat

Copy from:

- Anthropic
- Linear

Rule:

- keep the central conversation calm, dense, and structurally authoritative

### Cockpit

Copy from:

- Linear operational density
- OpenAI Platform honesty

Rule:

- make runs feel inspectable, not decorative

### Store and configurator

Copy from:

- ElevenLabs
- Notion card restraint

Rule:

- modular, premium, quiet

### Notifications and notices

Copy from:

- Google Material 3
- Linear inbox mindset

Rule:

- short-lived notices stay light
- durable intervention items stay inline and structured

## Benchmark Verdict

Empyralis should not chase novelty for its own sake.

It should combine:

- Linear’s density
- Notion’s calmness
- Anthropic’s structured AI framing
- OpenAI Platform’s operational honesty
- Material 3’s component discipline
- ElevenLabs’ premium creator polish

That mix is the correct north star.
